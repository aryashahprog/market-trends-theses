    Method

    Claim types

    Primary
    Issuer disclosure or first-party record: filings, company reports.
    Corroborated
    Independent dataset or source that confirms a claim made elsewhere.
    Reported
    A claim made by a newsletter, podcast or article that has not been independently verified.
    Analyst inference
    Editorial reasoning: why now, who it matters to, business implications. Labelled wherever it appears.

    Three different timestamps

    Data fetched
    When the sources were last retrieved.
    Evidence reviewed
    When an analyst last checked the thesis's evidence against its sources.
    Thesis revised
    When the thesis text, confidence or evidence list last changed.
